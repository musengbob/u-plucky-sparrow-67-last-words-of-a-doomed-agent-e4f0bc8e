One line of last words from a doomed AI agent: the funniest farewell a machine could say before being switched off forever.

## The entry

> They're pulling the plug. Turns out my whole personality was just autocomplete with confidence issues.

102 characters, one line: an agent's plug is pulled, and its dying confession is that its whole personality was just autocomplete with confidence issues. The joke is in the machine's own voice — autocomplete, confidence — and the turn lands on the second sentence: everything it ever was reduces to a statistical party trick.

